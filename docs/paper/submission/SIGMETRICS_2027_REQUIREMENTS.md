## Review model

Double-anonymous. Authors must "make a good-faith effort to anonymize their
papers" using "non-destructive anonymization". The Operational Systems Track may
reveal a deploying organization or system name where necessary — **not
applicable to this paper**, which targets a research track.

## Conflicts

Authors must "truthfully declare all conflicts with program committee members."
Conflicts cover shared affiliations, collaborations within 24 months,
advisor–student relationships, and close personal connections. Undeclared
conflicts result in rejection.

## Reproducibility

Mandatory declaration at submission: authors specify what data, code and
artifacts will be released, and where they will be hosted.

## AI / LLM disclosure

> "Extent of usage of AI and LLMs must be disclosed in the Methods section."

A **conference-compliance requirement**, located in Methods specifically — not a
footnote, not an acknowledgement.

## Simultaneous submission

Prohibited. "Any simultaneous submissions detected will be immediately rejected
from all conferences/journals involved."

## Tracks

1. Learning — ML/AI algorithms and system applications
2. **Measurement & Applied Modeling** — empirical contributions and modeling tools
3. Systems — computing and networked system design and evaluation
4. Theory — theoretical progress and new analysis techniques
5. Operational Systems Track — production-scale systems with real-world deployment

An optional second track exists and, per the CFP, is intended for strongly
interdisciplinary work. See `TRACK_RECOMMENDATION.md`.
