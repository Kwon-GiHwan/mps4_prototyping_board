## Central Control (line 1115-1116)
The Central Control (CC) is the main control unit inside the NPU. The CC controls how the NPU
processes neural networks, maintains synchronization, and handles data dependencies.
## NPU_SET_BLOCKDEP semantics (lines 6246-6248)
NPU_SET_BLOCKDEP takes a block oﬀset k as a parameter. The block dependency guarantees
that IFM block read n in the kernel does not start until all OFM block writes of the previous kernel
operation, except max(k-n, 0), are complete and written to memory.
